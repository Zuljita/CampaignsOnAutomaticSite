Where the app stands

  • Reading is done first and deepest. The app opens whole vaults, tolerates incomplete ones, and renders the dashboard, atlas, notes, relationship map, quest and commission boards, search, timeline, health and problems views.
  • The write lanes are deliberately few: the player-safe player/ export, one-click reveals, the campaign clock, faction goal checkmarks, and minting tonight's session note. Every one is a compare-and-swap edit that fails closed.
  • Bring your own editor. The app reads and renders; it has no text editor in it. The assumed companion is Obsidian open on the same folder — see how the two sit together. Neither app is bundled with, or required by, the other.
  • When builds ship they will be unsigned, like the rest of the family's early access — expect the once-per-download first-launch challenge from your OS, with the same steps the Hexes releases page documents.
  • Questions and requests go to the family's public tracker — say it's Campaigns.
